Q: How do you know two channels belong to the same person?
A: The active group uses independent verified email/phone/LID evidence. Source identities remain separate. Matching a name is not sufficient and a LinkedIn connections preview creates no automatic identity binding.
Q: Which promise wording is supported?
A: Eligible records must be direct message authorship with extraction permission. The guard supports explicit English “I will”, “I'll”, “I promise to” with listed action verbs and French “Je vais”, “Je promets de”, “Je m'engage à” or supported first-person future forms, such as “J'enverrai”. It rejects questions, negatives and conditionals; quote/signature boundaries stop authored-span collection. Group/unknown content and unavailable text are ineligible. This is candidate screening, not complete multilingual intent understanding. Exact quote/action/date validation still runs, and only the owner confirms.
